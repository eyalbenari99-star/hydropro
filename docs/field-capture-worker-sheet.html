1. Open Nexi — Buksan ang Nexi

  1. Connect to farm Wi-Fi.
    Kumonekta sa Wi-Fi ng farm.
  2. Open the browser, type the Nexi link above.
    Buksan ang browser, i-type ang link sa itaas.
  3. Log in with your username.
    Mag-log in gamit ang username mo.
  4. Tap ALLOW for Camera and Microphone.
    I-tap ang ALLOW para sa Camera at Mikropono.
  5. Menu → Add to Home screen, so next time it opens with one tap.
    Para isang tap na lang sa susunod.

2. Report something — Mag-report

  1. Go to Production → 🥽 Field Capture.
  2. Tap the greenhouse (GH1…GH16, NUR, PACK…).
    I-tap ang greenhouse.
  3. Type the table / line — example T3.
    I-type ang table o linya.
  4. Choose what it is about: Production, Maintenance, Quality, Irrigation, Packaging, Note.
    Piliin kung tungkol saan.
  5. Choose how serious: Info · Watch · Urgent.
    Gaano kaseryoso.
  6. 📷 Take photo — point and shoot.
    Kunan ng litrato.
  7. 🎤 Speak feedback — talk normally, Nexi writes it. Or type it.
    Magsalita lang, isusulat ni Nexi.
  8. Tap the big green ✅ SAVE TO NEXI.
    I-tap ang berdeng SAVE.

3. Faster way — QR sticker

  • Every table has a QR sticker. Scan it — Nexi opens already tagged with that GH and table.
    I-scan ang QR — nakatakda na ang GH at table.
  • Then only: photo → speak → SAVE. No typing.
    Litrato → salita → SAVE. Walang type.

4. Rules — Mga tuntunin

  • One problem = one capture. Do not put 3 problems in one photo.
    Isang problema = isang capture.
  • Photo close enough to see the leaf / the part.
    Malapitan ang kuha.
  • Say what you see, where, and since when.
    Ano, saan, at kailan pa.
  • Urgent = water stopped, pump down, big damage. Call the radio too.
    Urgent = tumigil ang tubig, sirang pump, malaking pinsala. Tumawag din sa radyo.
No internet? Photos are still saved and upload by themselves when signal comes back. Voice needs signal — type the note instead.
Walang internet? Naka-save pa rin ang litrato, mag-a-upload mag-isa kapag may signal. Ang boses ay kailangan ng signal — i-type na lang.
Care of the glasses / Pag-aalaga: charge every night · clean the lens with cloth only · never leave in the sun or in the FR room · return to the supervisor at end of shift and sign the logbook.
I-charge gabi-gabi · basahan lang ang panlinis · huwag iwan sa araw o sa FR room · isauli sa supervisor at pumirma sa logbook.
Questions / Katanungan: Production Supervisor · IT · Nexi Help. Everything you save goes straight to Nexi with your name, the location and the time.
